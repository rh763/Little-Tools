How it works

Every piece is an implicit solid: a smooth outer shape (band, arc or ellipsoid) intersected with a triply periodic minimal surface (TPMS). The pattern is wrapped around the piece, so the cells follow the curve of the band instead of being cut from a straight block.

Sheet thickens the surface itself into a thin wall. Network fills one side of the surface, giving a strut-like lattice.

Edge rails add solid borders along the band edges (or a shell around the earring), which makes the piece much stronger and nicer to wear.

Choose a surface from the list or type your own formula in x, y, z. One period of 2π in each direction is one cell.

The mesh is built with marching cubes and can be simplified. It is always closed and watertight. Weight is estimated from the mesh volume and the chosen material.
